Summary sections · Digest

S1 · Digest

Sentences, for reading rather than working through. A digest tells the reader how things stand so they can decide whether to read the rest of the page. Nothing in it is dismissible, because it describes state rather than asking for anything; it can be collapsed, regenerated, or it goes away on its own. See collapse, dismiss, or neither for the three behaviours.

Shared anatomy

Every summary section is an instance of the Container component (Sales Cockpit · Container). The variants below only change what goes in the Body. Toggle each example between the two container props.

Variants

Three jobs, one shape. Line marker and footer are the only things that change.

S1a · Status digest

Summary

As of today · 15 workspaces, 1,240 devices
All 15 workspaces are active and 1,198 of 1,240 devices reported in the last 24 hours.
42 devices have not reported for more than 7 days, most of them in Edge-North-Fleet.View devices
No subscriptions are due for renewal in the next 90 days.

Job: "Tell me how things stand right now." Three or four lines that say what is fine and what is not, so the reader can decide whether to read the rest of the page.

Use when
Someone lands on an overview after a gap and needs the state of play before the widgets. Mixed news: some good, some not.
Not when
Something is on fire. A critical finding wants a pill and a row (S2), not a sentence between two calm ones.
  1. Content header: heading "Summary", subtitle "As of today · population"
  2. Trailing: collapse chevron (36px icon button); folds body, header stays
  3. Line: status dot 10px · sentence with bold subject · optional link right-aligned
  4. Three to four lines, 10px apart, Footer not used
  • Order lines in status order: critical, warning, ok, unknown; never more than one ok line
  • Subject first, in bold, with the figure in it ("All 15 workspaces", "42 devices")
  • A link only where there is a page to go to; good news gets none
  • One fact per line; no semicolons joining two findings
  • Say "All" when a count is the whole population
  • Default: 3 to 4 lines
  • Minimum: 1 line (still worth showing if it is amber or red; drop the section if the only line is ok)
  • Maximum: 4. More than four facts is a findings list, not a digest
  • Collapsed: header row only, chevron pointing right
  • Not dismissible. Collapse folds to the header and persists per user
  • Lines leave when the condition clears
  • Frame 800 wide, hug height; line grid 10px | 1fr | auto, gap 12
  • Dot vertically aligned to the first text baseline (top 1px)
  • Component props: lines (1–4), each with status, text, link?

S1b · Change digest

Since you last signed in

12 days ago · 15 workspaces
18 devices were added, all in Optima Bank.
2 subscriptions expired: Compute Ops Management on Alpha Bank and Edge-North-Fleet.
MFA was enforced in 3 more workspaces; 12 still have a gap.
4 users joined and 1 left.
Compared with your last sign-in on 29 Oct.View all activity

Job: "What happened while I was away?" Only deltas since a fixed point: last sign-in, last week, last release. Steady state is left out.

Use when
Returning users, weekly reviews, release notes for an account. Pages people visit on a cadence rather than live.
Not when
First visit or very frequent visits; there is nothing to compare against, or the list is empty most of the time.
  1. Content header: heading "Since you last signed in" (or "This week"), subtitle "N days ago · population"
  2. Trailing: collapse chevron
  3. Line: trend icon 14px (ink colour) · bold quantity · what and where
  4. Footer: comparison point left, "View all activity" right
  • Deltas only; anything unchanged is left out
  • Trend icon says direction, not goodness: additions are up, expiries are down. No colour
  • Group by object (devices, subscriptions, security, users), one line each
  • Lines with no direction (joined and left) take no icon
  • Footer names the exact comparison point (a date), not "recently"
  • Default: 3 to 5 lines
  • Empty: section not shown (no "nothing changed" line)
  • First visit: section not shown
  • Collapsed: header row only
  • Not dismissible. Collapse folds to the header
  • The whole section resets to the new comparison point on each visit
  • Line grid 1fr with inline 14px icon and 4px gap before the bold quantity
  • Footer 14/20, flex space-between, no hairline
  • Component props: period label, lines (1–5) with direction (up / down / none), text; footer date

S1c · Generated narrative

Insights Beta

Generated 2 minutes ago from the last 30 days
Device onboarding is running at about 40 a week, double the rate in September. Most of the new devices are in two workspaces, Optima Bank and Alpha Bank, which together now hold 61% of the estate.
Subscription use is uneven: 9 workspaces are on evaluation terms, and none is due to renew in the next 90 days, so there is time to consolidate before the next cycle.
Why did onboarding speed up?Which workspaces are under-licensed?Compare with last quarter
Generated from usage and subscription data. Check figures before acting on them.Was this useful?

Job: "Explain this data to me." Prose that interprets the numbers below it: trends, concentrations, causes. It adds meaning; it does not list findings.

Use when
Analytics and usage pages where the charts need a reader's commentary, and the product has a model behind it.
Not when
Compliance, security or billing, where a sentence that is slightly wrong does damage. Anything a user must act on today.
  1. Content header: heading "Insights" with a Beta tag, subtitle "Generated N minutes ago from the last 30 days"
  2. Trailing action: "Regenerate"
  3. Body: two short paragraphs, bold figures inline
  4. Prompt chips: 2 to 3 follow-up questions
  5. Footer: source and disclaimer left, "Was this useful? Yes · No" right
  • Interprets, does not list: trends, concentrations, causes. If a paragraph is a finding, it belongs in a findings list
  • Two paragraphs, 40 to 60 words each, maximum
  • Figures are rounded and bold; "about 40 a week", not "39.6"
  • Disclaimer is one sentence and always present
  • Prompt chips are questions, in the user's voice
  • Default: 2 paragraphs + 3 chips
  • Generating: header stays, body shows a 3-line skeleton, chips hidden
  • Failed: one-line "Could not generate insights" with "Try again" in the footer slot
  • Feedback given: the Yes · No pair is replaced by "Thanks"
  • Not dismissible; "Regenerate" replaces the body
  • If the model is off for an account, the section is absent, not empty
  • Beta tag 11/600, 1 6, r4, --info-bg
  • Chips 12.5, 4 10, r14, 1px --line border, gap 8, wrap
  • Component props: generated-at, paragraphs (1–2), chips (0–3), feedback state (idle / given)
