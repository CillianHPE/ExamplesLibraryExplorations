Summary sections

Most overview pages open with a few lines that say what matters before the widgets do. There are three shapes, and each has three jobs. Pick by asking what the reader came to do; the shape follows. The research and critique behind this is on the exploration page.

Pick a shape

Three questions, in order.

1 · Read or work?

Is the reader here to understand the state of things, or to do something about it? Read: Digest. Work: a list or cards.

2 · Facts or tasks?

Are the items things that are true (a gap, a date), or things to finish (renew, enforce)? Facts: Findings list. Tasks: Cards.

3 · Does the list end?

A fixed set that completes is a checklist or steps. An open set that refills is triage, deadlines, actions or opportunities.

The three shapes

S1 · Digest

"Tell me how things stand."

Sentences with a status dot or trend icon. For reading. Never dismissible; hides or regenerates.

  • S1a Status digest
  • S1b Change digest
  • S1c Generated narrative

S2 · Findings list

"Show me what needs doing."

Rows with a pill or tick and one action each. The default for overview pages. Dismiss per row, never critical.

  • S2a Triage list
  • S2b Deadline list
  • S2c Progress checklist
Open spec

S3 · Recommendation cards

"Do these things."

One card per task with its own controls. Three or fewer. Room for a consequence or benefit and a quiet second action.

  • S3b Opportunity cards
  • S3c Getting-started steps

Rules that hold across all nine

Copy

  • Title names the job, not the shape.
  • Subject leads every line; the figure is in it and bold.
  • Actions are links, verb-first. Never an instruction written as prose.
  • "All" when a count is the whole population. Abbreviations spelled out.

Dismissal

  • Per item, not per section, unless every item is optional.
  • Critical is never dismissible. It leaves when fixed.
  • Dismissed goes somewhere: "Show dismissed (n)".
  • Sections that describe state offer "Hide", which collapses to the header.

Placement

  • One summary section per page, under the content header, above the stat strip. 32px to each.
  • If two jobs compete, the critical one takes the slot and the other moves to the rail.
  • Uncontained on the body; grey container inside a dashboard column.
