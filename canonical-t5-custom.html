T5 · Custom board

Job of the page: the user's own cockpit: they choose what matters, we provide the parts. Unlike T1–T4, there are no fixed content regions: everything below the header is a widget on a grid. Use for: "My dashboard", role-based home alternatives, team boards. Secondary rail: none: rail content (status, quick actions, copilot) is offered as widgets instead.
