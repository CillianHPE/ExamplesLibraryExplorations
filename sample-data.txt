About DemoComp |  | 
DemoComp Industries is a leading manufacturer of industrial automation equipment and smart logistics solutions. With production facilities across Europe, North America, and Asia-Pacific, DemoComp serves customers in automotive, aerospace, pharmaceuticals, and consumer goods sectors. The company operates a global supply chain network with over 200 distribution centers and edge computing sites. |  | 
Why HPE GreenLake |  | 
DemoComp requires unified infrastructure management across hybrid cloud environments, edge computing sites, and on-premises data centers. HPE GreenLake provides the as-a-service flexibility, centralized management, and consumption-based model that aligns with DemoComp's digital transformation strategy. Key use cases include Compute Ops Management for server fleets, Aruba Central for networking, Private Cloud Enterprise for mission-critical workloads, and edge-to-cloud data services. |  |
