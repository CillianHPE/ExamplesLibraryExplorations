Archetype 4 variant — Analytics explorer with KPI cards as the metric switcher. Instead of a metric dropdown in a query bar, the three summary cards act as tabs: selecting one re-renders the chart, legend and breakdown table for that metric (kgCO2e / kWh / USD). Shared time range and filters apply to all metrics; collected vs. projected split is consistent everywhere (solid past, shaded future). Evidence: HPE GreenLake Sustainability insights, Stripe, Vercel & Cloudflare analytics. Rule: the cards are the same "query" — only the lens changes, so selection state must be obvious and the chart/table must never disagree with the selected card.
