A3 · Figure column

Numbers form a right-aligned column (tabular numerals) with descriptions reading rightward, like a mini table without rules. The headline's label moves beside it, so every line follows one grammar: number, then meaning.

Scans fastest when there are 2–4 breakdown rows; scales to more rows better than side-by-side stats.

A4 · Prose-led

The whole story is one sentence with the figures set in heavier, larger type. No layout scaffolding at all; typography is the only structure.

Reads most naturally, but numbers are hard to compare across cards and localisation gets harder. Best for narrative or AI-summary surfaces.
