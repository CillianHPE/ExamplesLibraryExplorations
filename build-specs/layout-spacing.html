Layout spacing scale

Three sizes from the HPE design system spacing scale govern page rhythm at the macro and meso levels. Pick by relationship, not by eye: the further apart two things are in meaning, the bigger the token.

xxlarge
64 px
Macro · Between the primary content area and the secondary content area. The largest gap on any view; used once.
large
32 px
Macro · Between content sections within a content area: separates one topic from the next.
small
16 px
Meso · Card sibling gap: between containers / cards inside a content section, and between stacked modules in the secondary area.

Rules of thumb: xxlarge appears exactly once per view, between the primary and secondary content areas. A content section's internal grid uses small for both row and column gaps. The secondary area has no sections of its own, so its cards stack at small. Never mix sizes inside one grid, and never invent an in-between value: if a gap wants 24 px, the grouping is wrong, not the scale.
