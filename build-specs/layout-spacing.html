Layout spacing scale

Three semantic tokens govern vertical and horizontal rhythm on a page. Pick by relationship, not by eye: the further apart two things are in meaning, the bigger the token.

--space-region
64 px
Between the primary and secondary regions of a page. The largest gap on any view; used once.
--space-section
32 px
Between content sections within a region: separates one topic from the next.
--space-container
16 px
Between containers inside a section: cards in a grid, stacked modules in the rail.

Rules of thumb: --space-region appears exactly once per view. A section's internal grid uses --space-container for both row and column gaps. The secondary rail has no sections of its own, so its modules stack at --space-container. Never mix tokens inside one grid, and never invent an in-between value: if a gap wants 24 px, the grouping is wrong, not the scale.
